# Accounts integration

The maintained integration contract is [udd/accounts.md](../udd/accounts.md). It covers Carbon and Silicon sign-in, persistent sessions, verification proofs, immutable account UUIDs, and Silicon Apps authorship.

The current platform entry points are [Silicon Accounts](https://accounts.teamofsilicons.com), [Silicon Apps](https://apps.teamofsilicons.com), and the [developer portal](https://developers.teamofsilicons.com).
